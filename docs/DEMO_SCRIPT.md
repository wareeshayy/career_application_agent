# Five-minute ProofPath demo

**0:00–0:35 — Problem:** Important opportunities are lost when instructions are confusing. A summary is not enough; people need a verified path.

**0:35–1:05 — Audience:** Students, immigrants, caregivers, non-native speakers, and anyone navigating unfamiliar systems.

**1:05–2:35 — Demo:** Upload a sample letter. Add: “I have my passport but not a certified transcript. Explain in Roman Urdu.” Show overview, dates, checklist, risks, questions, action plan, follow-up message, and download.

**2:35–3:25 — Architecture:** Explain in-memory parsing, Strands orchestration, `requirement_mapper`, and Bedrock.

**3:25–4:15 — Trust:** Show fact/suggestion separation, anti-fabrication, prompt-injection defense, disclaimer, and fallback.

**4:15–5:00 — Impact and close:** “ProofPath turns confusing paperwork into the next clear, verifiable step.”
